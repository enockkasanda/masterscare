ABOUT MASTERS CARE

Developing infrastructure with purpose.

OUR MISSION

Transforming waste into energy, resources and value.

To develop and advance transformative waste to energy and resource recovery infrastructure that responds to Africa's evolving environmental, energy and economic needs.

Masters Care brings together project development expertise, innovative technologies and strategic partnerships to structure and advance projects from concept through implementation and long-term operation.

Our mission is to create sustainable, commercially viable and scalable infrastructure that transforms waste into energy, resources and economic value while delivering meaningful environmental and social impact.

OUR VISION

Building a sustainable African future.

To be a leading African project development company driving the transition towards integrated, sustainable and resource efficient waste and energy infrastructure.

We envision a future where waste is recognised as a valuable resource, where innovative infrastructure supports cleaner and more resilient cities, and where the recovery of materials and energy contributes to sustainable economic growth.

Through our projects and partnerships, Masters Care seeks to contribute to an African economy where environmental responsibility, innovation and economic development work together to create lasting value.

A WORD FROM THE CEO

Yanga Tsengwa

Chief Executive Officer | Masters Care

At Masters Care, we believe that the future of waste management lies in how effectively we can transform waste from an environmental challenge into a valuable resource.

Across Africa, growing urban populations, increasing waste volumes and rising demand for energy are creating a clear need for infrastructure that is more integrated, efficient and sustainable. Addressing these challenges requires more than traditional waste management. It requires innovation, accountability and a long-term approach to infrastructure development.

This is the space in which Masters Care operates.

We focus on developing waste-to-energy and resource recovery projects that bring together technology, infrastructure, investment and strategic partnerships. Our role is to identify opportunities, structure projects and coordinate the capabilities required to progress complex developments from concept through to implementation.

01

Innovation

Innovation is central to how we think and operate. We believe Africa requires solutions that are not only technologically advanced, but also relevant to the realities of the markets and communities in which they are implemented.

We seek to incorporate appropriate technologies, progressive development models and strategic partnerships that enable waste to be converted into energy, recovered materials and economic value.

02

Accountability

Accountability defines how we approach our responsibilities.

We recognise that major infrastructure projects have long-term implications for governments, communities, investors, partners and the environment. We therefore place strong emphasis on responsible project development, transparency, professional standards and measurable outcomes.

OUR APPROACH

Partnership-driven infrastructure development.

Masters Care understands that transformative infrastructure cannot be developed in isolation. Successful projects require the alignment of municipalities, government institutions, technology providers, engineering and construction partners, investors, operators, environmental specialists and other stakeholders.

We therefore operate through a partnership-driven model, bringing together the right expertise and capabilities around each project to create solutions that are technically sound, commercially viable and environmentally responsible.

Our work extends beyond waste-to-energy. Through resource recovery initiatives, including the processing of recovered plastics into recycled pellets, we are contributing to a more circular economy in which valuable materials are recovered, transformed and returned to productive use.

As we continue to develop our portfolio, our focus remains firmly on creating infrastructure with purpose—projects that respond to Africa's environmental and energy challenges while contributing to economic development and long-term sustainability.

At Masters Care, we do not simply develop waste projects. We develop solutions that turn waste into energy, resources and lasting value.
